VACUUM PUMPS FOR MILKING MACHINES

Our vane pumps provide a capacity of 200 to 3100 litres per minute at 50 kPa.
We supply vacuum units with a tank or base, with or without an electric motor, complete with accessories or as a standalone pump.
We also stock spare parts and accessories for milking systems.
